Lead:

Run for free through Lucerne’s stunning scenery, led by a passionate local runner. Ideal for travelers who want to stay active, explore the city, and meet like-minded people in a small group. Choose your pace and route, listen to local stories along the way, and experience Lucerne from a runner’s eye view.

Touren: (in einem Karussel o.ä. dargestellt)

Good Morning Scenic Run:

Join a relaxed early-morning run through Lucerne's stunning scenery. Discover the city’s most iconic sights and start your day feeling refreshed.

Suitable for all levels of runners

5-6 km, flat route, moderate pace (6:00-6:30 min/km). Start: 07:45 - finish around 08:30.

Optional: join for a well-deserved coffee afterwards

Classic Heritage Run:

Explore Lucerne’s rich history on a scenic run connecting the city’s most famous landmarks. Discover the stories behind Lucerne's icons.

Suitable for runners with some experience.

7–8 km, flat route, moderate pace (6:00–6:30 min/km). Starts: 11:30 and 17:30.

Hidden Gems Run:

Discover Lucerne’s hidden treasures on this special route. A perfect way to see the city from a new perspective and uncover ist old and new local secrets.

Suitable for runners with some experience.

8-9 km, flat route, moderate pace (6:00–6:30 min/km). Starts: 11:30 and 17:30.

Top Views Challenge:

Less talk, more elevation. Enjoy a rewarding run with three scenic viewpoints offering breathtaking panoramas of Lucerne and the lake.

Suitable only for experienced runners.

9–10 km, elevation gain 250-300 m. Start: 17:30.

About your guide

I’m a passionate local runner who loves trail and city runs, padel, and mountain biking. Born and based in Lucerne, I know the city’s streets, hills, and hidden corners inside out. I’m fluent in English, German, and French.

My vision is to offer runners of all levels and backgrounds a free, unique, and entertaining way to explore Lucerne together.

Allgemeine Besonderheiten von Running Tours:

Effizienz: Man sieht in kürzerer Zeit mehr von der Stadt als bei einem Spaziergang.

Gesundheit: Bewegung und Sightseeing werden auf angenehme Weise kombiniert.

Einzigartige Perspektive: Man erlebt die Stadt aus einer dynamischen und aktiven Sichtweise.

Lokaler Kontakt: Die Guides sind oft Einheimische und können interessante Einblicke geben.

Flexibilität: Oftmals können individuelle Wünsche und das Tempo der Gruppe berücksichtigt werden.
